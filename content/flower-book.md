## 📚 故事总结 / Story Summary

### 完整故事 / Complete Story
一粒葵花籽在春雨里喝饱水，拱开泥土顶掉种壳，长成有绒毛和高个子的向日葵，清晨开出金黄花瓣和深褐花盘——那其实是上百朵小花挤在一起。秋天，被一千多粒种子压得低头的花盘，把种子交给了风和鸟，其中一颗已经在旁边拱出了新芽。
A sunflower seed drinks its fill in the spring rain, cracks open the soil and shrugs off its shell, grows into a tall hairy sunflower, and opens one morning into golden petals around a dark disk that is really hundreds of small flowers packed together. In autumn the head, bowed by more than a thousand ripe seeds, hands them to the wind and a bird — and one of them has already sprouted nearby.

### 延伸活动建议 / Extension Activities
1. **种一颗种子 (Plant a seed)**: 和孩子一起在杯子里种一粒葵花籽或豆子，浇水、放在温暖处，不必晒到太阳也能发芽；发芽后再移到光下 (Plant a seed in a cup, water it, keep it warm — it starts without sunlight; move it to the light once it sprouts)
2. **画生长五格 (Five-frame growth strip)**: 用五个格子画出种子、发芽、长高、开花、结籽，讲给家人听 (Draw seed, sprout, tall plant, flower, seeds in five boxes and tell the story)
3. **数花盘 (Count the disk)**: 秋天看真正的向日葵，从外圈往里数小花，找一找螺旋；观察成熟花头朝哪个方向 (In autumn look at a real sunflower, count florets from the edge inward, find the spirals, and see which way the ripe head faces)
4. **风的实验 (Wind experiment)**: 对着一株低头的花或一把干籽轻轻吹气，看种子怎样落回土里 (Blow gently on a bowing head or a handful of dry seeds and watch them fall back into the soil)
